Welcome to the
INET '98 Network
http://www.inet98.ch
The network infrastructure of the INET '98 conference is supported and provided by a University of Geneva engeniering team. This team consists mainly of undergraduate students of the Computer Science Department. We will be happy to answer all technical questions you might have.

To facilititate your work, we have set up this website. It contains F.A.Q., software, hardware and network descriptions and is updated constantly as new issues arise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Please use the Q&A Forum to ask questions and find the most recent updates and information, post comments and suggestions. Your feedback is very important - INET '98 is a unique opportunity for us to share ideas and learn a lot from people that are the core of the Internet development.
