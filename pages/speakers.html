Overview

This page describes the available software and hardware for speakers. If you are a speaker, please read this page carefully. You will find the network infrastructure description here.

For unanswered questions, please refer to the Q&A Forum at http://www.inet98.ch/forum or (for urgent questions only) contact an IT support team member at Bureau 4.

Speakers Ready Room

The equipment of the Speaker Ready Room is an exact match to the one at the Conference Rooms where presentations are effectively made. You can configure your portable to work together with the LCD Projector in the SR room from 7:30 AM to 6:00 PM.

Preparing your Conference

You can connect a portable PC or a portable MAC to the INET '98 Network in the Conference Rooms in order to make your presentation. For detailed documentation about connecting your portable, please refer to configuring your portable section.

Server file storage is avaialble for speakers on all PCs in the Speaker Ready Room and in the Conference Rooms. (Note that it is NOT available on Conference Rooms MACs.) You can use the shared drive configured on these machines under letter S:. You may copy all files needed for your presentation in the Speaker Ready Room and find them in the Confenrce Rooms on the same S: drive.

Note that this shared disk is writeable by any speaker. We strongly advise you to identify your files in an intelligent way to avoid confusion. For instance you could use the date of the session and a session name abreviation. Please create subdirectories.

Best practice is to copy files to the shared S: drive in the Speaker Ready Room, turn off and turn on the PC in the Conference Room before each presentation to obtain a clean hard disk image and copy the files on the shared S: drive to hard disk C: for the session itself.

Please do not delete somebody elses files. No accounts will be created.
